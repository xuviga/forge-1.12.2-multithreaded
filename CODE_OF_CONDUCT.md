# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.

## Our pledge

We want a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of
experience, identity, background, or hardware.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and technical.
- Assume good faith.
- Critique code, not people.
- Provide reproduction steps and logs.
- Respect licensing and intellectual property.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or hate speech.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Encouraging piracy or distributing proprietary game files.
- Spam or repeated off-topic posts.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that violate
this code. Repeated violations may result in a ban.

## Scope

This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any project space.

## Reporting

Report violations privately to the maintainers. Do not escalate in public
threads.
